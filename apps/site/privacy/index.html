Privacy

sosmn.org is run by volunteers of the Science of Spirituality Minneapolis Meditation Center to share our events. We keep only what we need to run them.

What we keep

  • When you sign in with Google or Microsoft: your name and email address. We never see your password.
  • Details you add: the name you'd like us to use (we also keep the name from your sign-in), an optional ZIP code so we know roughly where visitors come from (never a street address), an optional phone number, and whether we may text or WhatsApp you about events.
  • Your journey with the center (volunteer admins only): which programs you've come to, whether you've chosen to share finished Copilot challenges, and short notes that help us welcome you. Never anything about your inner experiences.
  • Your responses: Going, Maybe or Can’t go, how many guests you’re bringing, and when you checked in.
  • Which event pages you opened while signed in, so we can plan and follow up. Visitors who aren’t signed in are only counted.
  • Notes you leave for the center after checking in to an event. Only admins read them. A note is shared publicly (first name only) only if you tick that you allow it, and an admin chooses to.
  • Your favourites (events and series you saved). They are just for you: admins can’t see them.

Who sees it

Only Minneapolis Center admins, to organise events, send reminders and help you. Volunteers who check people in at the door see names only. Every admin look-up is recorded. We never sell or share your details.

Your choices

You can change your RSVP or details at any time (👤 → Your details), including “Please don’t send me any messages from the center”. To have your details removed, email MinneapolisCenter@sos.org.

Location

This site serves the Minneapolis area. Visitors from outside Minnesota are offered our page on sos.org; we use only the approximate region your connection reports, and we don’t store it.

Science of Spirituality’s own privacy policy applies to sos.org. Meditation Copilot (prototype) has its own privacy notice.
