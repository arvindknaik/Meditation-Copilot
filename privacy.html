Privacy Notice

Meditation Copilot · Last updated: 9 November 2026

Draft for review. Meditation Copilot is a prototype and is not an official or approved Science of Spirituality app.

Summary

  • Your sittings, reflections and roadmap answers belong to you.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your personal data, and we do not show ads.
  • We do not run a server that stores your meditation data.

Where your data is stored

Guest mode: everything is stored only in your browser on this device. It is never sent to us. It can be lost if browser data is cleared, the app is removed, or the device changes. You can export a CSV backup at any time from Insights.

Signed in with Google or Microsoft (when available): your data is saved as a private file in your own Google Drive (in a hidden app folder) or OneDrive (in Apps/Meditation Copilot). The app asks only for access to its own folder, not to your other files. Google or Microsoft store that file under their own privacy terms. You can delete it at any time by removing the app's access in your Google or Microsoft account settings.

Sign-in information

When you sign in, Google or Microsoft confirm your identity to the app in your browser. The app uses your account only to reach its own folder in your drive. We do not receive or store your password. A short-lived access key (and, for Microsoft, a renewal key valid for about a day) is kept on your device so the app can sync; signing out removes it.

Anonymous usage statistics

If enabled, the app counts general events such as "app opened" or "sitting saved" using a cookie-free analytics service, to understand overall usage. These counts contain no reflections, names, email addresses, birth dates or other personal details. You can turn this off under Account & settings.

Diagnostics for support

The app keeps a short technical log on your device (for example "sync completed" or an error message) for up to 90 days. It never contains your reflections or personal details. It is shared only if you tap Send diagnostics to support, which lets you email or share the file yourself.

Links to other sites

Links to sos.org, WhatsApp and other sites open those sites, which have their own privacy policies.

Not medical advice

Meditation Copilot is not a medical or mental-health service. Please consult a health professional about any health concern.
