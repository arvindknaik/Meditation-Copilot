## New

* 21-Day Foundation in Copilot: sit 10, 15 or 20 minutes every day for 21 days. When you finish, continue or take the 21-Day Advance, growing to 30 minutes.
* When you start using Copilot, you choose your first challenge: the 21-Day Foundation or attending 6 sessions in 3 months.
* What's new page, and the version number in the footer.
* admin: Follow-ups: people to thank, check on or welcome back, with ready-to-edit messages.
* admin: Journey: everyone's level (New, Seeker L2–L4, Initiate), drifting, and a person page with timeline, mentor, notes and milestones.
* admin: Event type and "mainly for" on every event, and an Insight tab (opened → going → came, first-timers, came back).
* admin: Challenge settings: minute choices, grow-to, next step, offered at sign-up.
