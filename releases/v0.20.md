## Highlights

* Copilot: reading and video challenges, with books by the Masters and talks to watch.

## New

* Copilot reading and watching challenges: Daily Thought (30 days), an article a week, a book a chapter at a time, and talks (3 a week). Catching up is always allowed.
* Copilot: "✓ I read it" on the Thought for today, "Mark as read" on Learn articles, book chapters with a finish celebration (+100 points), "✓ Watched" for talks, and "Your reading" in Learn.
* Copilot: when you finish a challenge you can choose to let the center know (only its name and date are shared).
* sosmn.org: an icon for each event type (Public Intro, Workshop, Satsang, Retreat, Darshan, Festival, Celebration), or a small picture of the flyer.
* sosmn.org: "All our programs are free" on the event lists, and FREE on every event.
* sosmn.org: each event says whether a children's program is offered.
* sosmn.org: Visitor guidelines page (video monitoring, photography, quiet during meditation, children, check-in).
* Your details: editable name, optional ZIP code, "Please don't send me any messages", and a short privacy note.
* admin: Library tab: books (look up details from an Amazon link or ISBN), talks and article links; only approved items appear in Copilot.
* admin: Challenges: new Education type (what to read or watch, pace, total, points each), and four starter reading/video challenges.
* admin: People stages (Potential, New, Seeker, Satsangi, Initiate) and status (Active, Drifted, Dropped, Passive, Declined), Sevadar flag, ZIP code and both names on the person page.
* admin: Seeker orientation progress (5 workshops, 2 challenges); Follow-ups for "orientation done" and "ready to be Satsangi?".
* admin: Events: one Audience (Public, Community, Initiates) instead of "Who can see it" and "Mainly for"; children's program checkbox; event types updated.
