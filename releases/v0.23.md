summary: An easier start on Today, a calendar with Upcoming and Recent lists, favourites, notes for the center, and the 10,000-hour journey for initiates.

## Highlights

* Copilot: start from Today with "Getting started": the quote of the day, a quick tour, your daily sitting time and more.
* Calendar: Upcoming and Recent lists, ☆ favourites, and a private note for the center after you attend.
* Initiates: the 10,000-hour journey is now an optional, lifelong challenge with a target year.

## New

* Copilot opens on Today with "Getting started": read the quote of the day, see who's on Copilot, start the timer, set your daily sitting time, take the tour, see upcoming events, start a challenge. Each gets a ✓; hide it any time (Account → Getting started brings it back).
* A 5-card tour of Today, Timer, Calendar, Roadmap and Learn (also in Account and Learn → Help).
* Your daily sitting time opens straight from Today, and Today shows your next sitting with "▶ Start timer".
* Calendar on sosmn.org: Upcoming (yesterday through the next 30 days, by day, with "Show the next 30 days"), Recent (2 to 32 days ago, with "Show earlier", up to a year back) and Month. The buttons jump to the next date with events.

* Home page: 4 upcoming events (★ featured first, then by date) and 2 recent events. Copilot's Today shows the same.
* ☆ Favourite an event, or save a whole series (every date of a repeating event). See them under Calendar → ★ Favourites and in Copilot's Calendar.
* After checking in, leave a note for the center on the event page (one per event, until 7 days after it). Only the center team reads it; tick the box if the center may share it (first name only).
* 10,000-hour journey for initiates: begin it from the Roadmap, choose a target year, and Copilot recommends the daily time that gets you there. Your initiation date and Master carry over; no date of birth needed.
* No questions at sign-in: everyone starts as a seeker. Initiated? Tell Copilot once on the Roadmap ("I've been initiated"); initiates' challenges, such as the 10,000-hour journey, then show first. This is one way: there's no switching back to seeker.
* admin: Several events can be ★ Featured. They stay featured, under Recent events once over, until you remove the ★.
* admin: Visitor log: notes people leave after checking in, with "Reviewed", "Public" (only when they allowed sharing), search and CSV.
* admin: Suggestions from Copilot, with "Reviewed" and CSV (earlier ratings are kept).
* admin: Settings: Copilot's "Featured today" talks, and the center check-in poster to print.
* admin: Challenges: "For" is now Everyone / Not yet initiated / Initiates only. Initiates-only challenges show first, with an "Initiates" tag.
* admin: People: confirm a self-declared Initiate, or revoke it (they become a Seeker and can't declare it again in Copilot).

## Improved

* The calendar always opens on Upcoming; the address keeps the tab and how far you've loaded, so Back and shared links work. Home: "Recent events" has "See more ›".
* "Thought for today" is now "Quote of the day" everywhere (the Daily Quote challenge too).
* Copilot's footer has one link back to sosmn.org.
* No first challenge is forced on new users; choose one on the Roadmap when you're ready.
* Roadmap: one list of challenges in a clear order (Your challenges · Initiates only · Meditation · Attend the center · Read & learn · Create your own), and every challenge starts the same way: one sheet with what it asks, its length and points, the one choice it needs, and Start.
* Copilot now asks everyone to sign in with Google or Microsoft. Sittings already on a phone in guest mode are added to the account at the first sign-in.
* Account & settings is simpler: Contact details, Send a suggestion. The old in-app admin pages are gone (everything is on the admin site).
* Public reflections on events are replaced by private notes for the center; earlier reflections moved into the visitor log.
* admin: The admin site always loads its newest version after a release (no manual refresh), and the menu has its own row on a computer.

## Fixed

* The app no longer pops up a request to rate it.
