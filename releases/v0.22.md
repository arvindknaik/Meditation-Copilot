## Highlights
- Learn: articles, talks, books and quizzes in one place, with one search (quotes too).
- New quizzes to check your understanding, with points for trying and passing.
- ✓ marks what you've finished, ◐ what's in progress, and ★ Save keeps your own list.

## New
- Learn shows the top 5 articles, talks, books and quizzes, with "See all" lists you can filter by topic and status and sort.
- Search finds the best articles, talks, books, quizzes and quotes; narrow to one type with the chips.
- Quizzes: 10 questions, shuffled each time; pass with 8. Passing shows every answer; otherwise review the questions you missed and try again after a short wait. After 3 tries all answers are shown.
- Quiz points: 5 for the first try and 2 for each try after; passing adds 25 (1st try), 15 (2nd) or 10 (3rd).
- ★ Save for later: your own list that stays until you unsave it (also for favourites).
- Continue: books you're reading, quizzes you're working on and saved things not yet done.
- Talks have their own page with a picture, "Watch" and "✓ I watched it"; Related items under every article, talk and book.
- New challenge: Pass 5 quizzes in 60 days.
- admin: Library: full article text editor, topics (up to 3), 📌 Pin to the top 5, quizzes with a question editor and a Review screen to approve them.
- admin: Eleven draft quizzes written from published SOS sources, each question with its source, ready for review.
- admin: Anonymous counts per item (opened, finished, passed). No names are recorded.

## Improved
- The Learn articles that were built into Copilot are now in the Library, so admins can edit them; everything you read keeps its ✓.
- The app's own help articles moved to "Help with the app".
