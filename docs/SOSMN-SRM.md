# SOSMN-SRM — Seeker Relationship Management

**Science of Spirituality Minneapolis Meditation Center**
Draft 2 · November 25, 2026 · Owner: Arvind

> Purpose: help every person who comes to the center take their next natural step in meditation and spiritual life, at their own pace, with a real person who knows them. Nothing is sold. Success means people learning to meditate, practicing regularly, and feeling at home at the center.

---

## 1. Who we serve

| Group | Who | Journey |
|---|---|---|
| **New** | Need an introduction: invited, interested, or came once | Level 1 |
| **Seekers** | Attend programs; exploring meditation, not yet initiated | Levels 2–4 |
| **Initiates** | Members who have received spiritual initiation | Initiate track (section 4) |
| **Guests** | Brought by someone; no contact details yet | Become *New* if they choose to hear from us |

Names decided 2026-11-25: **New**, **Seekers**, **Initiates** (not customers, not necessarily members).

---

## 2. Principles

1. **Service, not sales.** The system suggests a next step; a volunteer decides whether and how to reach out.
2. **Each person's pace.** Levels are a guide, not a ladder anyone is pushed up. No deadlines, no rankings.
3. **We track participation, never inner life.** Attendance, challenges and lessons can be recorded. Devotion, surrender and experiences are never scored or noted.
4. **Easy to stop.** Anyone who signs up receives event announcements by default; every email has an unsubscribe link, and people can change their choices any time. At most about one message a week.
5. **Practice data stays private.** Copilot sittings stay on the person's device unless they choose to share a summary.
6. **Least access.** Hosts see names and check-in only. Journey levels, notes and follow-up lists are **admins only**. Notes are short and never sensitive.
7. **Their data, their control.** People can see and delete what the center keeps; inactive records are cleaned up after 2 years.
8. **Aligned with SOS.** Check with sos.org on any organization-wide policy for contact data before this grows.

## 3. The Seeker journey

### Level 1 — Introduction
**Goal:** a newcomer understands who we are and can sit for meditation.

**What they learn**
- Science of Spirituality (SOS) and Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj
- High-level teachings: meditation on the inner Light and Sound
- The center: facility, volunteer-run, all programs **free**, family friendly
- Purpose and benefits of meditation
- The technique: how to sit for meditation

**How they get it**
- Intro session / open house (event type: *Introduction*)
- Welcome pack: links to the About page, a short video, "how to sit" guide (Copilot Learn)
- A volunteer host greets them and becomes their contact

**Signals the system sees**
- Invited, opened an event, RSVP'd, first check-in
- Copilot account created; first sitting logged (if shared)

**Ready for Level 2 when** they have attended at least one introduction and have sat for meditation at least once.

**Center actions**
- Thank-you message within 48 hours of the first visit, with the next workshop date
- RSVP'd but didn't come → "sorry we missed you" with the next date

---

### Level 2 — Going deeper
**Goal:** meditation becomes a habit and questions get answered.

**Activities and targets (Arvind's criteria)**
| Activity | Target | How it is tracked |
|---|---|---|
| 21- or 42-day meditation challenge | Start and complete one | Copilot challenge (center challenge or guided 42-day) |
| Weekly interactive workshops | **6–8 sessions within 3 months** | Check-ins at events typed *Workshop* |
| Learning articles / lessons | **1–2 per week** | Copilot Learn (lessons read), with consent |
| Open questions on the teachings | **None left open** | Question log (new feature, see 6.4) |

**Topics:** teachings in more depth, refining the technique, common challenges (restless mind, sleepiness, time) and tips to handle them.

**Signals:** workshop count in the last 90 days, challenge progress, lessons read, questions asked/answered.

**Ready for Level 3 when** 6+ workshops in 3 months, one challenge completed, and no open questions.

**Center actions**
- Congratulate challenge milestones (day 7, 21, 42)
- Nudge after 3 weeks without a workshop
- Host follows up on any unanswered question within a week

---

### Level 3 — Regular practice
**Goal:** regular satsang and a strong daily practice.

**Activities**
- Attend regular meditation sessions (**Satsangs**)
- Continue challenges: a number of hours per week/month
- Start an **early morning** meditation habit (Amrit Vela)
- Grow toward **60 minutes of meditation daily**

**Tracked through:** satsang check-ins; Copilot *hours* and *Amrit Vela* challenges; center challenge "60 minutes daily for N days".

**Signals:** satsang attendance per month; weekly sitting days and average minutes (only if the seeker shares a summary).

**Ready for Level 4 when** attending satsang regularly (e.g., 2+ per month for 3 months) and practicing most days.

**Center actions**
- Invite to retreats, conferences and special programs
- Gentle "we've missed you" after 60 days without a visit

---

### Level 4 — The spiritual Master, seva and devotion
**Goal:** understand the role of the spiritual Master and the spirit of selfless service.

**Activities**
- Learn the role of the spiritual Master
- Experience **Darshan** of the Master when possible (events, conferences, tours)
- Understand **seva** (selfless service): take part in center seva
- Develop devotion, surrender and resilience — *personal and never measured*

**Milestone:** has **met Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj in person** (recorded by an admin, or reported by the seeker).

**Tracked through:** attendance at Darshan/special events; the "met in person" milestone; seva sign-ups (new: seva roles/shifts).

**Center actions**
- Share upcoming Darshan opportunities and travel information
- Offer seva roles matched to interests (greeting, setup, kitchen, youth, tech, outreach)
- Information about the initiation process **when the seeker asks** (initiation is handled through SOS; the center does not decide readiness)

## 6. Features for admin.sosmn.org

### 6.1 Journey view
- Each person shows **Seeker L1–L4** or **Initiate**, calculated from signals; an admin can adjust it by hand.
- Overview: count at each level, moves up this month, and who is drifting.

### 6.2 Person page
- Timeline: invitations, events opened, RSVPs, check-ins (by type), guests brought, challenges, lessons (if shared), questions.
- Assigned **host/mentor**, short volunteer notes (no sensitive details), contact preferences and unsubscribe status.
- Visible to **admins only**.

### 6.3 Follow-up list (daily)
First-timers to thank · RSVP'd-not-came · challenge milestones · unanswered questions · drifting regulars · ready-for-next-level.
Each item has a ready-to-send email/WhatsApp message and a "done" tick (builds a contact log). **Admins only.**

### 6.4 Question log
Seekers ask in Copilot or on sosmn.org; hosts answer or arrange a conversation; marked answered. Supports the Level 2 "no open questions" goal. Frequently asked questions can become Learn articles.

### 6.5 Audience suggestions
For a new event: suggested people by level, interest and last visit; respects message limits and consent.

### 6.6 Event insight
Opened → RSVP → came; first-timer share; returned within 30 days; workshop series completion (6–8 in 3 months); source of each visit (WhatsApp, email, flyer QR, Meetup, Facebook).

### 6.7 Practice insight (opt-in)
- Center totals by default (no names): active meditators this week, challenges completed, lessons read.
- Per person only through the seeker's **Roadmap report** (section 9): trends and summary, shared by the seeker with their mentor/admins. Requires a privacy notice update first.

### 6.8 Seva roster *(later)*
Roles, shifts, sign-ups, thank-yous.

## 10. Copilot Challenge Program (spec, 2026-11-25)

One program of challenges in Copilot, managed from admin.sosmn.org → Challenges. Every challenge has a **fixed end** (a date or a count) — never open-ended. When one ends, the seeker can sign up again and choose the length.

### 10.1 Challenge kinds

| Kind | Example | Counting | Misses |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Meditation (daily sitting)** | 21-Day Foundation; 21-Day Advance | Minutes sat each day | Allowed (time-bound): 1 per 10 days |
| **Attendance** | 6 sessions in 3 months | Check-ins at center events | Count by the end date |
| **Education · Quote** | Daily Thought, 30 days | Quotes read | **Catch-up allowed** before the end; no misses needed |
| **Education · Article** | 1–2 articles a week, 12 weeks | Articles read | Catch-up allowed |
| **Education · Book** | A chapter or two a week until the book is finished | Chapters read; **finishing the book is celebrated** with bonus points | Catch-up allowed |
| **Education · Video** | 3–5 videos a week, 4 weeks | Recommended videos watched | Catch-up allowed |

Rule: **time-bound** tasks (sitting on a given day) allow misses; **count** tasks (reading, watching, attending) can be caught up any time before the end, so they need no misses.

### 10.2 Meditation path (replaces the single 42-day challenge)
1. **21-Day Foundation** — first priority. Sit daily at the same chosen time and length.
2. Near day 21, Copilot offers:
   - **Another 21 days** at the same time and length, or
   - **21-Day Advance** — add about 1 minute a day on most days, reaching **30 minutes** by day 21 (more is welcome; reaching 30 is celebrated).
3. Foundation + Advance together = the former 42-day challenge. People already in a 42-day challenge finish it as is.

### 10.3 Sign-up
- In Copilot, everyone **chooses at least one challenge during sign-up** (not skippable):
  - **21-Day Foundation** (meditation), or
  - **Attend 6 sessions in the next 3 months** (needs Google/Microsoft sign-in, because check-ins live on the server).
- Afterwards, a "Join more" card suggests the Education challenges, in the admin's priority order.

### 10.4 Content managed in the admin site (new **Library** tab)
- **Articles:** built-in Learn articles plus links the admin adds (e.g., sos.org articles), with a short summary in our own words.
- **Books:** authored or authorized by SOS Masters. The admin pastes a purchase link (Amazon or other seller); title, author and cover are filled in automatically where possible and always reviewed before publishing. The admin adds the **number of chapters**.
- **Videos:** recommended talks and satsangs (YouTube or sos.org links). Replaces/extends "Featured videos".
- Every item is **approved** before seekers see it.
- **Quotes** stay in the app (76 approved quotes) for now.

### 10.5 How Copilot counts "read" and "watched" (on the phone)
- **Quote:** "✓ I read it" on the Thought for today card; missed days' quotes can be read later to catch up.
- **Article:** "Mark as read" at the end of an article (enabled after scrolling to the end); outside links count when the seeker confirms on return.
- **Book:** choose a book → "I'm reading this" → tick chapters as read → "Finished" (celebration + bonus).
- **Video:** opens the video → "✓ Watched" on return.
- Reading and watching history stays on the phone; the center sees it only through the seeker's Roadmap report.

### 10.6 Book club (next step after the book challenge)
- A **Book Club** links a book, a chapter-per-week plan and a weekly discussion meeting (an event on sosmn.org, type *Study / Book club*).
- Members join from the event or Copilot; their book challenge follows the club's weekly plan; meeting attendance counts as check-ins.
