Welcome! Our center is a quiet place for meditation, open to everyone. All programs are free of charge. A few simple guidelines help everyone feel safe and at peace.

  • 📹 Video monitoring. For safety and security, the center premises are monitored by a video surveillance system.
  • 📷 Photography. Photos and video recording are not allowed on the premises without permission. Please ask a volunteer first.
  • 🔕 Quiet during meditation. Please set your phone to silent and keep conversation for before or after the program.
  • 👪 Children. Children are welcome. If an event doesn't offer a children's program, please keep children with you.
  • 🙏 Check in. Scan the QR code at the welcome table or check in on the event page, so we know who is here.

Questions? Please ask a volunteer at the welcome table, or email MinneapolisCenter@sos.org.
