Science of Spirituality

Science of Spirituality is a global, nonprofit, spiritual organization dedicated to personal transformation through meditation, under the direction of Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj.

From its centers in countries all over the world, Science of Spirituality strives to help individuals and communities achieve deeper, richer inner lives with a stronger sense of meaning and purpose.

We are a global, nonprofit, spiritual organization. But we are much more than that. We are millions of individuals, of all nationalities, races, and faiths, who are deeply committed to bettering ourselves and our world through a spiritual way of living based on meditation, ethical values, and regard for life.

We welcome you to join us at our conferences, meetings (satsangs), workshops, talks, retreats, and meditation seminars, always held free of charge at our center.

Through Sant Rajinder Singh Ji Maharaj’s teachings, his guidance, and his example, millions of people around the world are discovering the beauty and power of the soul.

Learn more at sos.org.

Directions

From the north (I-694 east or west)

  1. Take exit 38 for Central Avenue (MN-65).
  2. Go south on Central Avenue to 41st Avenue NE.
  3. Turn right onto 41st Avenue and go one block.
  4. Turn left onto Van Buren Street, then right into the parking lot. The center is on your left.

From the south (I-35W north)

  1. Take exit 21B for Johnson Street in Northeast Minneapolis and continue north on Johnson Street.
  2. Turn left onto 27th Avenue NE, then right onto Central Avenue (MN-65).
  3. Turn left onto 40th Avenue NE, then take the first right onto Van Buren Street NE (a very short block).
  4. The center is on your left at the end of the block. Turn left into the parking lot.
