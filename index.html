⚠️ Before you continue as a guest

As a guest, your sittings, reflections and roadmap are stored only on this device. They may be lost at any time, for example if:

  • browser data or history is cleared,
  • the app is removed or you change phones,
  • on iPhone, Safari removes website data after about 7 days without use (adding the app to your Home Screen helps).

Export a CSV backup regularly from Insights, or sign in later to keep your data in your own drive. Your guest data can be moved into your account when you sign in.
